Thank You For Your Order!

Your mail-in sharpening request is registered. We have pre-composed your estimate email draft — please make sure to send it from your mail app so we can match your package. Follow the three steps below to ship us your blades.

01

1. Package Safely

Wrap each knife blade in heavy cardboard or several layers of bubble wrap. Tape it tightly so the blade cannot shift. ⚠️ Safety first: ensure tips cannot pierce the packaging!

02

2. Label Package

Print and attach the mailing label Matthew sends to your contact email. Write your return address clearly on the exterior box.

03

3. Ship Package

Drop off your package at any USPS post office location, or hand it to your local mail carrier. We'll alert you the moment it arrives!
