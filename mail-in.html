Safety Packing Guidelines
  • 1. Guard the Tips & Edges

    Fold thick cardboard over the blades or wrap them in multiple layers of newspaper. Securely tape the cardboard sheath around the blade so it cannot slip off during transit.

  • 2. Bundle & Cushion

    Roll the sheathed blades together inside bubble wrap or thick packing paper. Place them in a sturdy shipping box or heavy-duty padded bubble envelope.

  • 3. Enclose Your Contact Info

    Place a printout of the packing slip (generated below) inside the box. If you don't have a printer, write your Name, Phone Number, and Shipping Address on a piece of paper and tuck it inside.
