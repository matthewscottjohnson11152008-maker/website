Our Story

Meet the Craftsman

My journey with edge tools began far from West Michigan. Having been born in Zeist, Netherlands, I grew up with a deep appreciation for classic craftsmanship, attention to detail, and quality design. When I relocated, I carried that same European dedication to work standards and precision with me.

Now at 17 years old, I have directed my focus and dedication toward business and local entrepreneurship. Vanguard Sharpening was founded not just as a business, but as a commitment to serve others by breathing new life into the tools they rely on daily.

I believe that a sharp edge is more than a convenience—it is a mark of respect for the tool and the craft. Whether you are a culinary enthusiast preparing meals for your family, a gardener tending to local soil, or a woodworker crafting custom designs, my passion is to provide you with reliable, prompt, and premium sharpening service.

Core Principles

Precision

Applying progress-whetstone techniques and individual tooth inspection for flawless, razor-sharp edge restoration.

Service First

Focusing on convenient local Grand Rapids drop-off and flexible mail-in logistics to serve every client's schedule.

Entrepreneurship

Building a transparent, flat-rate business model driven by passion, hard work, and continuous improvement.
