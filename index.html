WHAT WE DO Our Services We offer the full range of professional sharpening techniques, matched to what your blade actually needs. Grindstone Heavy-duty bevel reprofiling for chipped, cracked, or severely worn blades. Water-cooled throughout to protect the steel’s temper. Whetstone Hand sharpening on Japanese water stones, progressing through calibrated grits from 400 to 12,000. The go-to for any blade that needs a sharp, clean edge. Full Restoration Grindstone reprofiling followed by full whetstone progression. For blades that haven’t been maintained in years or have sustained real damage. Blade Types Kitchen knives · hunting & pocket knives · chef’s knives · cleavers · chisels · scissors · specialty blades. If it has an edge, we can sharpen it. HOW IT WORKS The Process Every blade goes through the same careful three-step workflow — no shortcuts. 01 Intake & Inspection Your blade is examined under magnification. We assess the steel, current geometry, and any damage before recommending a service. 02 Grindstone Where needed, the grindstone resets the primary bevel, removes chips, and restores the tip — water-cooled to protect the steel throughout. 03 Whetstone Progressive grit stones refine the edge from coarse to ultra-fine.
