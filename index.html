What We Do

Our Services

We offer the full range of professional sharpening techniques, matched to what your blade actually needs.

Grindstone
Heavy-duty bevel reprofiling for damaged or severely worn blades. Water-cooled to preserve temper.
Whetstone
Precision hand sharpening on fine Japanese whetstones. Restores a clean, razor-sharp edge to any blade.
Rolling Sharpener
Angle-guided system with diamond and ceramic rollers. Maintains a precise 15° or 20° edge for ultimate consistency.
